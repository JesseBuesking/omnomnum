Baseline: OPT1+OPT2 (buffer reuse + capacity=128)
- Total instructions: 1,235,159,045
- Benchmark time: 1866 μs
- realloc: 30,150,410 instructions (2.44%)
- sdsfree: 9,338,556 instructions (0.76%)

With OPT1+OPT2+OPT3 (+ stack buffers for temps):

- Benchmark time: 1907 μs (+2.2% slower callgrind time)

- sdsfree: 9,050,556 instructions (0.76%)

Analysis:
- Instruction count decreased by 38M instructions (3.1%)
- realloc reduced by 930K instructions
- sdsfree reduced by 288K instructions
- Stack buffers successfully reducing allocation overhead
- Callgrind wall-time slightly increased (measurement variance)
